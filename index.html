Insulinfo

Due to a limited amount of insulin producers being in the market, the companies that produce insulin are able to charge very high costs to maximize their profits. In order to help people get access to affordable insulin, this site exists to help provide information about where to get access to coupons or lower cost suppliers.


There are several sites that offer coupons for or lower-cost access to insulin. Below are brief descriptions of several:

GoodRX:

GoodRX is healthcare company that hosts a website for comparing drug prices across pharmacies and providing coupons for lower cost perscription drugs. Currently, "Insuin Lispro", "Insulin Glargine", "Insuline Spart", "Insulin Degludec", and "Insulin Lispro 75/25" are listed on the site. The site provides a coupon that can be presented at your pharmacy for a discounted price.

TrumpRX:

TrumpRX is a program started by President Trump in 2026 to help offer Americans lower cost access to perscription drugs. Currently "Insulin Lispro", "Insulin Glargine (U-300)", and "Lantus" are all listed on the site. The site provides a coupon that can be presented at your pharmacy for a discounted price.

ADCES Cost-Saving Resource Guide

A guide for finding cost-saving information on for a variety of insulin manufacturers created by the Association of Diabetes Care & Education Specialists. Copay card links are provided per manufacturer and avaliability information is provided based on insurance enrollment.

Lantus Savings:

Similar to the Lilly Insulin Value program, Lantus Savings and Support is a program offered by Lantus for providing $35/month supplies of insulin. In order to use this program, consumers must be perscribed an elligable Lantus product. Different optiosn are avaliable depending on your insurance coverage.

Cost Plus Drugs:

Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drugs Company is a company that sells drugs and related products at the cost it takes to acquire plus 15%, plus the pharmacy fee. Currently, they do not sell insulin itself but "Insulin Pen Needles" are listed on the site. Customers purchase their drugs from the site and it is shipped directly to them.

Theory

In the United States, three companies (Eli and Lilly Company, Novo Nordisk Inc., and Sanofi U.S.) produce almost all of the nation's insulin. Because of this oligopolization of the market, they have been able to raise prices year over year to absurd rates without insurance, coupons, or other cost saving measures. From an economic standpoint, the problems are twofold: there is not enough competition in production for insulin and the market is organized in a way where lower prices and coupons are avaliable, but only to those who spend time to look. The purpose of this website is to assist diabetics in their search for affordable insulin. While it is difficult to introduce more competition to the insulin market, especially because of the entrenched political power of the pharmaceutical industry, the additional information provided through this website helps decrease the information gap between buyers and sellers.

About us!

This website is the deliverable for our group's Economics capstone at Willamette University in Salem, Oregon. Go bearcats!

We believe that lifesaving medicines should be accessable to all people and this site was designed to help fill the informational gap so that consumers can have access to lower cost insulin. This site is mostly a sample for a compilation of useful resources related to helping people find affordable insulin options.
