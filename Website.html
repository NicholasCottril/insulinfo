Insulinfo

Due to a limited amount of insulin producers being in the market, the companies that produce insulin are able to charge very high costs to maximize their profits. In order to help people get access to affordable insulin, this site exists to help provide information about where to get access to coupons or lower cost suppliers.


There are several sites that offer coupons for insulin. Below are brief descriptions of several:

GoodRX:

GoodRX is healthcare company that hosts a website for comparing drug prices across pharmacies and providing coupons for lower cost perscription drugs. Currently, "Insuin Lispro", "Insulin Glargine", "Insuline Spart", "Insulin Degludec", and "Insulin Lispro 75/25" are listed on the site. The site provides a coupon that can be presented at your pharmacy for a discounted price.

TrumpRX:

TrumpRX is a program started by President Trump in 2026 to help offer Americans lower cost access to perscription drugs. Currently "Insulin Lispro", "Insulin Glargine (U-300)", and "Lantus" are all listed on the site. The site provides a coupon that can be presented at your pharmacy for a discounted price.

Lilly Insulin Value Program:

Lilly Insulin Value program is a program offered by Eli and Lilly for offering Lilly insulins for $35 with or without insurance. In order to use this program, consumers must be perscribed an elligable Lily Insulin Value product, must be a US resident, must be 18 or older, and cannot be enrolled in ANY public healthcare program. After downloading a savings card, the program provides coupons that can be presented at your pharmacy for a discounted price.

Cost Plus Drugs:

Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drugs Company is a company that sells drugs and related products at the cost it takes to acquire plus 15%, plus the pharmacy fee. Currently, they do not sell insulin itself but "Insulin Pen Needles" are listed on the site. Customers purchase their drugs from the site and it is shipped directly to them.
