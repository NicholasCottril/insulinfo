About us!

This website is the deliverable for Professor Knight's Economics capstone at Willamette University in Salem, Oregon. Go bearcats!

We believe that lifesaving medicines should be accessable to all people and this site was designed to help fill the informational gap so that consumers can have access to lower cost insulin. This site is a sample for a compilation of useful resources related to helping people find affordable insulin options.

Project work done by Allison Ruskell, Elise Lien, Nicholas Cottril, and Tyler Chapman
